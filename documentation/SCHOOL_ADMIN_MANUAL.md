# DICBO School Manager - School Administrator Manual

## 1. Getting Started
Once your school is registered on the platform, log in with your primary administrator account.

## 2. Onboarding Wizard
The first time you log in, you will be guided through:
- **Identity**: Upload your logo and set your school motto.
- **Academic Setup**: Define your current term and academic year.

## 3. Managing Staff
- Go to **HR > Staff Directory**.
- Create accounts for Teachers and Accountants.
- Note: Staff will receive their temporary password via the platform or your internal communication.

## 4. Student Admissions
- **Add Student**: Navigate to the Students module to register learners. 
- **Admission Numbers**: These are auto-generated based on your school's unique counter.
- **Photos**: You can upload learner photos for digital identification.

## 5. Fee Management
- **Fee Structure**: Before recording payments, define how much each class pays in the **Settings** or **Finance** module.
- **Record Payment**: Accountants can capture payments and generate instant printable receipts.

## 6. Attendance
- Teachers can mark attendance daily from their own portal.
- You can monitor the school-wide attendance participation rate from your dashboard.

## 7. Reporting
- Access the **Reports** center for:
    - Enrollment summaries.
    - Revenue collection reports.
    - Academic performance (Future update).
