# DICBO School Manager v1.0.0 — Administrator Manual

## 🏫 1. Institutional Setup
### 1.1 Complete the Onboarding Wizard
When you first log in, you will be prompted to enter:
- **School Identity**: Name, Motto, Vision, and Logo.
- **Academic Structure**: Current Term and Academic Year.

### 1.2 Configuration
- **Classes**: Define your classes (e.g., Year 1 Alpha) under the Classes module.
- **Subjects**: Set up your curriculum (e.g., Mathematics, English).

## 🎓 2. Learner Admissions
- Navigate to **Students > Add Student**.
- Fill in the profile. The system will automatically assign a unique Admission Number.
- **Search**: Use the search bar to find students by name or admission ID instantly.

## 💰 3. Financial Operations
### 3.1 Defining Fees
- Go to **Finance > Fee Structure** to set tuition and other costs per class.

### 3.2 Recording Payments
- Navigate to **Fees > Record Payment**.
- Select the student, enter the amount, and choose the payment method.
- **Receipts**: After saving, click "Print Receipt" to generate a professional DIT-branded document.

## 🗓️ 4. Classroom Management
- **Attendance**: Teachers can select their class and mark students as Present, Absent, or Late.
- **Exams**: Record marks under the Exams module to track academic progress.

## 🛡️ 5. System Security
- **Staff Accounts**: Manage your team under **HR > Staff Directory**.
- **Audit Logs**: Review the Audit Trail to see all critical changes made to the system.
