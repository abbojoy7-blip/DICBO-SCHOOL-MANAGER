# DICBO School Manager — Customer Login Guide

Welcome to your new School Management ERP. Follow these steps to access your account.

### Step 1: Open the Portal
Visit the school's official ERP link:
**https://erp.dit-international.edu**

### Step 2: Sign In
1. Enter your **Email Address** (provided by the school office).
2. Enter your **Temporary Password**.

### Step 3: Secure Your Account
Upon your first login, we recommend:
1. Navigating to **Profile / Settings**.
2. Changing your temporary password to a secure, private one.

### Step 4: Dashboard Overview
- Use the **Sidebar** to navigate between modules.
- Check the **Dashboard** for real-time enrollment and collection stats.
- Logout immediately when finished if using a shared computer.

### Need Help?
Contact your school’s IT Administrator or the Dot Inspiration Technologies support team.
